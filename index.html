About

I am a mathematics Ph.D. student at the University of Wisconsin-Madison, working at the intersection of mathematics and machine learning. My master's thesis developed machinery to study Laplace integrals with several phase functions, which appear in Bayesian statistics. My current work is on developing the consequences for singular learning theory. I have also worked on neural network interpretability, scientific transformers, manifold learning, and Bayesian models for biological data.
