Recently, I have come to the view that scaling mathematical research in the LLM era, especially for AI safety, needs to move faster than academia traditionally does. I have therefore decided to show my results at an earlier stage of the paper-development process than is typical. Here is the current status of the subproblems I have developed to the standard I am currently setting for displaying my work.

Affine Residues and Chamber Jumps for Hyperplane Arrangements

My master’s thesis generalizes singular learning theory to handle finitely many energy (loss) functions. The increased difficulty is the difference between single-variable and multivariable complex analysis. To handle multivariate residues, I develop a residue theory of the cohomology of hyperplane arrangements.

Acyclic traversal of affine hyperplane arrangements

While the residue paper provides a jump formula for contour integrals across faces of the affine hyperplane arrangements, I need to apply the jump formula many times to make remainder terms small. Naive approaches may jump cyclically, making the resulting iterated jump formula useless. Here I set up the purely affine-geometric problem of constructing a strategy that avoids such cycles.
