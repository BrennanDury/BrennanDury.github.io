About

I am a mathematics Ph.D. student at the University of Wisconsin-Madison, working at the intersection of mathematics and machine learning. In my master’s thesis, I developed machinery to study Laplace integrals with several energy functions, which appear in Bayesian statistics. My current work develops consequences for singular learning theory. I have also worked on neural network interpretability, scientific transformers, manifold learning, and Bayesian models for biological data.
