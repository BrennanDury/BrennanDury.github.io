  • Meromorphic Continuation of Archimedean Zeta Functions Expository paper, 2024.

    One of the key steps in singular learning theory is to show that the zeta function meromorphically continues to C. I show a proof of this step, and explain how elementary local properties of holomorphic functions can prove the result in the one dimensional case, while resolution of singularities more powerfully controls the local behavior in high dimensions.

  • Free Form Least-Squares Concept Erasure Without Oracle Concept Labels EleutherAI blog post, 2024.

    I worked on concept editing at Eleuther AI. Here I derive the least squares optimal edit to a random variable such that the new random variable is uncorrelated with a label random variable. In this version, we require the edit to be a function of the unedited representation, without access to the labels. Consequently, this edit eliminates any linearly represented information, and cannot inject any additional non-linearly represented information into the representation.

  • The Importance of Being Parameters Distillation reproduction study, 2023.

    Neural integral equations proposes a method for predicting trajectories based on modeling the physical system by an integral equation, and using the transformer to model the corresponding integral operator. The algorithm applies the transformer iteratively, and I noticed that if you unrolled the iterations, the full computational graph can be viewed (almost) as one large transformer. Then there were exactly three independent changes from the obvious approach: treat the evolving state vector like a video and train an autoregressive video transformer. I thought these changes needed to be tested independently to understand why the algorithm performed well. I found that the dominant contribution was due to eliminating the distributional shift between training and test time that occurs in methods that autoregressively sample, conditional on their previous outputs instead of the ground truth, at test time. Somewhat surprisingly, I also found a small benefit of applying the same transformer iteratively instead of letting the weights for different passes be untied.
